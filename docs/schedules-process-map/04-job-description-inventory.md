## Staleness verdict

- Nothing in the HR canonical set has been revised since the November 2024 upload except Construction Operations Specialist (Oct 2025). Underlying drafts are March 2024. The company reorganized acquisitions (Jul to Aug 2025), ran a RIF (2025), eliminated purchasing and the SVP Operations role, split construction East / West under two SVPs, and realigned asset management (Jul 2025). Every construction and acquisitions JD predates those changes.
- Asset Management is the exception: the AM team rewrote its JDs in late 2025 and early 2026.
- Eight JDs describe roles nobody holds (National VP Acquisitions, VP Operations, AP Manager, AP Specialist, Investment Analyst, LD Project Manager, Regional Project Admin, CS Administrator, plus the three purchasing titles).
