# Process map: history and goals

**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Sources:** Outlook (search on "process map", 100+ threads, Nov 2024 to Oct 2026), SharePoint (`Strategy / Sitetracker / Complete Workflow Project`, `Construction / 02. Process Mapping and Milestones`, `Strategy / z. Archive / Process Map`).

## Goals, as written at the start (May 2024) and still current

The core team's own list, from the 5/28/24 meeting notes:

1. Create accountability in project development and execution so teams advance projects, key questions get answered, and major decisions are reviewed and signed off.
2. Clarify roles, responsibilities, and decision-making authority.
3. Visibility into what, when, why, and how the team spends its time, so bottlenecks and resource gaps can be corrected.
4. A shared understanding of all the parts needed to execute a community, for the whole company.
5. Higher-quality onboarding and training; the process map "could serve as the way."
6. Technology that makes it easy to use and update, not "another task."
7. Feedback and buy-in from the team, while allowing for project-specific nuance.
8. Implement over time; set a timeline, commit to progress, and weigh it against other priorities and cost.

Goals added since:

- **2025 priorities (Dec 2024, Kurt):** Sitetracker / process map update with roles and responsibilities; training; user functionality; reporting and oversight.
- **Jan 2025 (fee business plan):** extend the map to cover client onboarding so fee-build communities follow the same process.
- **Aug 2024 problem statement:** "At DD Exit the plans and costs are too high." The map exists partly to force the right work before DD exit.
- **Oct 2025:** budget update cadence tied to process map milestones; define Level 1 / Level 2 schedules (scope, detail, who creates, who reviews).
- **Jan 2026 (Frank):** simplify for user-friendliness and utilization; realign to personnel changes; hand to Sitetracker; retrain; then build sub-schedules for reporting and planning.
- **Mar 2026:** timelines should live in one system, not Planner plus Sitetracker; a training plan with a go-to person other than Frank.

## What the map covers for asset management, and what it does not

The Level 1 map carries five leasing milestones (PMA complete, website live, property manager hired, on-site leasing start, lease stabilization). The full 6/19/25 map adds monitoring of rental market performance, third-party PM identification, pre-lease coordination, the model-home opening countdown (90 / 60 / 30 days), signage, leasing office setup, and first move-in. There is nothing after stabilization, and nothing on the AM operating cadence.

The August 2026 Property Management Playbook outline (41 sections in six parts, with a RACI per section and a completion tracker currently at about 2 percent) is the right source for the AM sub-schedule. The sections that map to community milestones are 2.6 concession strategy for new lease-ups, 2.7 new community opening process, 2.8 grand opening events, 2.1 and 2.2 lead and renewal management in Knock, 2.5 signage standards, and 1.2 hiring by position. Those should become the Level 2 AM schedule hanging off milestones 8.3 through 9.5. See `03-critical-date-calendar.md`.
