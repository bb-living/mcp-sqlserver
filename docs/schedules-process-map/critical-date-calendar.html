How the dates work

Each milestone has a predecessor and a duration in calendar days, copied from the Updated ST Process Map sheet sent to Sitetracker in March 2026. A milestone's forecast is its predecessor's actual date (or forecast, if not yet actual) plus the duration, unless someone has typed a forecast override. An actual date closes the milestone. Status is late when the forecast is in the past with no actual, and due soon when it falls inside the next 30 days. The Accepted LOI date anchors the whole chain, so a community with no dates at all shows template dates from 1/1/2026.

Fund and state follow the warehouse: BBLivingDW's community lookup carries one fund (B2R I, B2R II, Blackbird, Legacy, TAC+, Historical for divested communities) and one state per community; Fee Build is added for third-party builds. The fund and state filters above the Portfolio, Community and Next 90 days views are remembered on this device. Pipeline deals that have no fund yet show as Unassigned.

Two rules keep history from showing as late. The community status closes the stages behind it: Pre-development closes IC Memo and DD Period; Under construction closes everything through Land Closing; Lease-up closes through Land Development; Stabilized closes everything but the Stabilized & Disposition stage; Sold closes all. Those show as "Complete (by status)". Separately, a milestone with no actual that sits before a later milestone that does have an actual shows as no record rather than late.

Where the dates came from. Acquisition and entitlement actuals (loaded 2026-11-07) are the per-community columns of ST Process Map - Historical Communities.xlsx (Strategy site, Sitetracker / Complete Workflow Project). Land, construction and opening dates (loaded 2026-11-08) are the construction team's Planner task grids, exported weekly as <community> Pre-Construction Schedule_<date>.pdf in the Construction site (Reporting Documents / Claude Project Folder / Task Grid Schedules); the 10/29/2026 set was used, past dates as actuals and future dates as forecasts. Each community's notes say which file each date came from.
